How It Works

1. Citizen Reports Issue

A resident submits a quick form detailing the location and nature of the problem, with optional photo evidence.

2. AI Analyzes Complaint

The AI engine instantly extracts the category, determines the priority level, and generates a concise summary.

3. Complaint Routed to Department

The system automatically alerts the relevant municipal team (e.g., Water, Roads) on their dashboard.

4. Municipal Team Resolves Issue

Field officers update the ticket status, giving the citizen complete transparency until the job is done.
